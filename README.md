# GitaMind AI

GitaMind AI is a scripture-grounded personal guidance web app that helps users navigate everyday personal, career, and ethical dilemmas through the timeless teachings of the **Bhagavad Gita**.

It translates a user’s situation into practical, structured advice grounded in authentic teachings and references from [Vedabase.io](https://vedabase.io/).

> GitaMind AI is intended for reflection and general guidance, not as a substitute for professional medical, legal, mental-health, or financial advice.

## What It Does

Users can describe a difficult situation or decision in their own words and receive structured guidance that:

- Clarifies the underlying dilemma
- Connects relevant principles from the Bhagavad Gita
- Provides practical, actionable next steps
- Encourages thoughtful reflection and responsible decision-making
- References source teachings for further study

The experience is designed to make ancient wisdom accessible, relevant, and useful in modern life.

## Key Features

- **Personal dilemma support** — Guidance for relationships, identity, stress, and personal choices
- **Career and purpose guidance** — Explore duties, responsibilities, motivation, and decision-making
- **Ethical reflection** — Structure difficult moral questions and consider possible approaches
- **Scripture-grounded answers** — Responses informed by authentic Bhagavad Gita teachings
- **Vedabase references** — Source-based context for deeper learning and verification
- **Structured recommendations** — Clear summaries, principles, reflection prompts, and practical actions

## How It Works

1. A user submits a question or describes a situation.
2. The app identifies the relevant themes and emotional or ethical context.
3. The guidance is organized around applicable Bhagavad Gita teachings.
4. Authentic verses, translations, or references are used to support the response.
5. The result is presented as practical, structured advice rather than only abstract philosophy.
